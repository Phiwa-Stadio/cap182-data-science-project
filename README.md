##### *Client STADIOalot : E-COOMERCE and LOGISTICS*

## Part A : Motivation

### 1. Industry Context

E-commerce is not just about getting customers to buy; it is also about getting their orders to them efficiently and reliably. For a business operating at STADIOalot’s scale, small delivery inefficiencies can quickly become significant costs. With around **4.1 million active customers and 58 million orders per year**, improving delivery efficiency is an important part of becoming more profitable.

### 2. Existing Challenge

STADIOalot’s delivery performance is declining. On-time delivery has dropped from **93% to 88%**, while failed or repeat delivery attempts have increased from **4.1% to 6.7%**. These failed deliveries cost the business more and create frustration for customers.

The challenge is to understand **which deliveries are most likely to be late or fail before they happen**. STADIOalot already collects data on routes, delivery times, drivers, GPS and delivery outcomes, creating an opportunity to use this information more effectively.

### 3. Importance of Addressing the Problem

This problem affects both STADIOalot and its customers. Failed deliveries mean additional driver time, fuel and vehicle costs, while late or repeated deliveries can lead to a poorer customer experience. With an operating margin of only **1.9%**, even small improvements in delivery efficiency could make a meaningful difference.

### 4. Potential Contribution of Data Science

Data science could help STADIOalot move from reacting to delivery problems to **predicting them**. By analysing historical delivery data, a model could identify orders that are more likely to be delayed or require another delivery attempt. These insights could then support better routing, scheduling and delivery decisions.

### 5. Expected Benefits

A successful solution could help reduce failed deliveries, lower delivery costs and improve on-time performance. Most importantly, it could provide a practical way for STADIOalot to turn the large amount of data it already collects into better decisions and a better customer experience.
