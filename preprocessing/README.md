Part A:

Motivation
Industry Context

E-commerce depends on getting orders to customers quickly, reliably and affordably. For a retailer operating at STADIOalot’s scale, even small delivery inefficiencies can become significant costs.

Existing Challenge

STADIOalot’s delivery performance is getting worse. On-time delivery has fallen from 93% to 88%, while failed or repeat deliveries have increased from 4.1% to 6.7%. These failures cost the business more and create a poorer experience for customers.

Why It Matters

With around 58 million orders a year and a very thin 1.9% operating margin, reducing unnecessary delivery costs could make a meaningful difference.

Contribution of Data Science

STADIOalot already has years of delivery data, including routes, times, drivers, GPS and delivery outcomes. This project will explore whether that data can be used to predict which deliveries are most likely to be late or fail, allowing the business to intervene earlier and make better delivery decisions.

The potential benefits are fewer failed deliveries, lower delivery costs and a better customer experience.
